Draggable metrics and the guides layer

Hover a purple line: it turns green and shows its value. Drag it. Shift snaps to 10; with a line hovered, the arrow keys nudge it by 1 (Shift 10). The left and right lines are the side bearings. Dragging the baseline shows a ghost where the glyph will land, then moves every outline on release.
