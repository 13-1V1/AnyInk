Photos: one photo makes many letters (B9)

A new font in Simple. Open a photo: ANY-INK finds one part per letter and picks the paper. Click a part (or take the green-dashed guess), type its letter and press Trace it: the letter opens, the photo is moved and sized so the part sits on its lines, and the part is traced. Use it puts it in as one Undo step, spaced automatically, with the photo faded and locked under it. Letters from one photo share one size; an earlier letter that no longer matches it follows in the same step.
