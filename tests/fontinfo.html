Font details and Move the guide lines (B15)

Simple, with the fontDetails flag on, on a font at the 1,000-unit grid whose small n, u and m are written 30 % taller than the small-letter line (650 against 500), and H; all four use automatic spacing. The font name above (and Menu > Move the guide lines...) send the top bar's door events, which the two installers answer, as main.js will. In Font details, each field is one Undo step when you leave it; Copyright fills itself from Your name until you change it. Move the guide lines... shows the purple banner under the bar. There is no canvas here: the buttons below make the guides layer's drag (the press, the ticks, the release's settings step), so the release is the one whole-font step that refits the letters.
