Sketch: drawing on the letter (S2)

Simple, with the sketch flag on for this page only. Pick a sketch tool on the column (or press L, A, S, C, R; V or E for Select) and click on the paper. Hold Ctrl to turn the snaps off for a click, Shift is passed on. Enter, a double-click or Esc finish the piece; a second Esc goes back to Select. The tools are the real ones: each finished piece is one Undo step on the letter. ?standin=1 swaps in this page's stand-in (see the source's header), which proves only the wiring and the draw layer and keeps its pieces on this page.
