Brush (B8)

The real Characters page on the sample font, drawn by the real Navigator, with the flags as the app has them (brush on). Pick the Brush (B or N) and drag on the paper: the live picture under the pointer is perfect-freehand; on release the stroke is inked (simplify, fit-curve, a round nib swept along the skeleton, clipper2-ts union, refit) and put in the letter as one Undo step, "drew a stroke on ...". Each stroke is its own shape, tagged with its stroke id, its skeleton kept in glyph.anyink.nib.strokes. The readout measures the last stroke's live picture against its ink, as a share of the stroke width (the B8 limit is 2 %). The size slider stands in for the letter card's Brush section (its own track puts it there).
