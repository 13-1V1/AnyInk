Placement on the guide lines (B3)

One photo, one size: every letter named from a photo gets the photo's scale (the median of what each letter alone would need), so their ink weight matches; only a row written clearly bigger or smaller gets its own. Every letter sits its body on the writing line, wherever on the page it was written; tails (g, Q, a dropping f) hang below. Each row's writing line (dashed) slopes with the photo; a g or p whose bowl closes above a tight row line sits on the line. Each letter is then traced with the real auto-trace at that placement.
