Undo that never surprises (B2)

A bare editor on a font with two empty letters, Capital B and small r. Open a letter, trace a square into it (the real auto-trace apply, one Undo step like Use it), then press Undo. In Simple one press undoes the newest change wherever it is: on a letter the view moves to the changed letter in the same press; on the board it stays and offers Show me. Opening a letter is never a step. In All tools upstream's undo is unchanged, with its "Navigated to" steps, but looking at a letter never makes a saved font unsaved and never counts toward the 200-step limit; past 30 "Navigated to" entries the ones that only pass the view along leave. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y work too (the real keyboard handler).

Mode: Simple (?ui=simple) All tools (?ui=all)
