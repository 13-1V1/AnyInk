Spacing (B3)

The spacing engine on its own, with no editor. Each letter shows its left and right edges (purple lines), the space between ink and edge (shaded), and what kind each side is: straight, round or open, from the side table or, for letters the table doesn't know, measured from the ink. The gap rule: G is 8% of the small-letter height; straight sides get G, round 0.6 G, open 0.4 G, punctuation 0.6 G. The dashed box is the letter's photo: it moves with the ink.

A side is measured on the letter's body (the dashed purple lines): the i's dot drifted right and the g's tail hooking left don't set a side; they hang into the space beside the letter, but stop 0.1 G short of its width, so they never run into the next letter ("gg", "il"). A leaning hand is measured with its slant taken out (read from the stems; a letter that leans its own way by more than 2 degrees, by its own lean), so it is spaced like the same hand upright.
