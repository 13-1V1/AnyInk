Simple top bar (B6)

A bare editor on a font with two letters, Capital B and small r, in Simple with the coach flag on. The bar above is the real one (makeAppTopBar): the logo and step 1 go to the letters (the Overview, where the letter view's "← All letters" goes and B10 mounts the board, whatever the flags), Undo and Redo name the step they act on (with its letter when it isn't the one open: "Undo: added a rectangle on small r") and say why when greyed, the status pill shows its four states, More tools switches to All tools with the purple band (its Back to simple returns), Menu opens Simple's Menu. Every Undo and Redo press toasts (bottom right); a new change ends that toast (its Redo does nothing, and a newer toast may take its place). Resize the window under 1440 px to see the rail fold to "1 2 3". Steps 2 and 3 and the font name's door show only with their flags.
