Letter board (B10)

The real Overview page in Simple, drawn by the real Navigator with the board flag on (this page only, until the integration turns it on in features.js): the board of the Quick font, 58 letters in board order, one progress line and its bar, each section's count once it has a ready letter, a check mark once it is done, the toolbar. The font, "Brennan Hand", has letters in every state: B H r n o from a photo, a drawn with the brush, c only a stray line (Empty, as the letter card says), e with ink that B11's check (a stand-in here) says floats above the writing line, and every other letter empty. The rail's "1 Letters 7/58" and the Next line read the same letters.

Turn checks on: small e turns amber, the count drops to 6 and the filters show (All / Needs a look / Empty); Needs a look shows e's reason and its outlined fixes (they only log here). The filter he picks lasts only while he stays on the board: open a letter and come back, and the board is on All again; turn checks off and on, and it is on All (the filters hid). On Needs a look, "+ Numbers & symbols" goes back to All and brings the new section into view, washed green for a moment. Click a tile to open the letter; "← All letters" brings you back with that tile ringed green and in view. Type a letter, use the arrows and Enter (board_keys.js). "+ Numbers & symbols" adds the section as one Undo step; Undo and Redo are on the top bar. Finish the small letters fills every empty small letter in one step: "All small letters done. Your font can type words now." shows once.
